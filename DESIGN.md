# What Matters Built visual and interaction system

## Identity
Independent practice by Jerio: creative work, digital marketing, and business operations. The existing name becomes a compact, two-line wordmark. Keep the supplied favicon. Do not fabricate a logo, project, metric, or client outcome.

## Type and grid
Self-hosted Archivo regular and semibold. No decorative italics. Display 44–84 px only in the homepage hero; page H1 38–66, H2 28–44, H3 22–28; body 16; supporting 14; labels 12. Editorial paragraphs use approximately 40–65 characters per line. Tables and factual metadata may be denser.

Black #080808, neutral white #F4F4F4, red #FF3B30. No cream, purple gradients, floating ornamental shapes, or repeated rounded card surfaces. Main grid max 1500 px, desktop gutter 56 px, touch gutter 16–24 px.

## Interactions
- Header: quiet horizontal bar; hover, click or keyboard opens a full-width contextual menu. Work links preview original artwork. Escape and outside click dismiss. Mobile uses a scrollable disclosure menu with direct archive links.
- Hero: three explicit discipline controls switch original brand/web compositions and a clearly labelled operations outcome. Desktop pointer movement reveals layer separation. Touch users can tap or swipe. No timed autoplay.
- Selected work: sticky visual follows the project in the reading column. Mobile gets inline images with the same problem/work/status information.
- Method: one open step at a time, revealing the decision and useful output.
- Motion: transforms and opacity, bounded pointer movement, requestAnimationFrame for scroll progress. Content stays readable without scripts. Reduced motion removes entrances and movement but preserves every control.

## Reference observations
Motion the Agency: restrained header above dense grouped navigation, with a featured visual. Warhol Arts: layered artwork and changes in composition on scroll. Oddly Made: confident, sparse type and an image-led opening. Yaara Israeli: project work remains central, with compact contextual information. Logo resources inform reduction and legibility, not copying marks. AMO and Ancient Artifacts rendered blank during the available inspection; no behavior inferred from them. Logobook and Logosystem were accessible; several other logo directories were unavailable.

## Acceptance
Check 320, 390, 768, 1024, 1440, 1920 and 2560 widths. Check nav open/close, keyboard and touch selection, visible focus, project destinations, ongoing status, reduced-motion state, image loading, and content overflow. Save desktop, menu and mobile screenshots. Build and Astro diagnostics must pass before deployment.
