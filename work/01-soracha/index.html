Work / Soracha
OrganisationSoracha
RoleBrand identity & photography direction
CategoryBrand, Marketing
Delivered

No brand existed yet, no name recognition, no visual identity, no online presence. Building an F&B brand from literally nothing.

Work

Before locking any visual direction, I mapped what Soracha actually had versus what they thought they had. Rather than wait on a production budget that didn't exist, I shot the initial photography myself on an iPhone 16, and built the identity system in Photoshop and Canva so it could be extended later.

Work

A complete, service-ready brand foundation, identity, photography direction, and a social system the brand could launch on immediately. Live today as "Soracha, Matcha Slow Bar," running a flirty voice built on Gen Z internet humor ("Matcha for main characters only") out of Night Market Attackz and Food Center GrandCity in Balikpapan, carried mostly through short-form video.

Verify what a client actually has, and actually needs, before you start building.
