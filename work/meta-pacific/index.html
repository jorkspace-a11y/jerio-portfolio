Work / Meta Pacific

Meta Pacific

RoleBrand direction and website strategy
CategoryStrategy, Brand, Web & Conversion
Delivered

Two connected workstreams for the same Bali creative-technology brand: an assignment-based brand direction and a production website built to make the offer, work, and enquiry path clearer.

Context

Meta Pacific had broad capability across strategy, production, technology, and growth, but the public presentation needed a clearer position and stronger proof. The brand-direction assignment explored how that position could look; the website work turned the operating offer into a live, structured digital presence.

Scope
  • Brand direction, identity routes, campaign applications, and landing-page concepts
  • Website information architecture, content structure, responsive implementation, and conversion flow
  • Search foundations, analytics, lead routing, and production QA
Work

Brand-direction assignment: built and presented distinct visual routes, logo systems, campaign applications, and landing-page concepts using Meta Pacific's real photography and service context.

Work

Production website: structured and shipped the public site across services, solutions, work, field notes, resources, pricing, and contact paths, with mobile behavior and technical search foundations checked before release.

Outcomes
2Connected workstreams under one Meta Pacific case study
37Website pages covered by the recorded local SEO validation
Limitations
  • The brand-direction work was produced as an assignment and is shown as design exploration, not as a claim that every route was adopted as Meta Pacific's final identity.
  • Website validation records confirm the tested build and deployment state at the time of review; they do not imply ongoing control of later content or infrastructure changes.

The assignment and the website make more sense together when their roles stay explicit: one explored the direction, the other shipped the public system.
