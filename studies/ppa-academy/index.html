PPA Academy

Vocational Training / EducationMarketing, Growth

A SWOT, product-market fit, and growth/risk strategy deck, done independently rather than as commissioned client work.

Assumptions
  • Based on PPA Academy's public program offerings and market positioning as observed.
Problem

Vocational training providers in this space often expand program offerings faster than they validate demand for each one, spreading marketing spend thin across programs of uneven strength.

Research

Mapped the program lineup against visible enrollment signals and competitive alternatives to identify where demand looked strongest and weakest.

Analysis

A full SWOT plus product-market fit read against the existing program mix, surfacing which programs justified growth investment and which carried disproportionate risk.

Proposal

A growth and risk strategy prioritizing the programs with the clearest demand signal, deferring investment in the weaker ones until validated.

Limitations
  • Not implemented — this is a strategic exercise, not a delivered engagement.
  • No access to actual enrollment or financial data; based on public signals only.
