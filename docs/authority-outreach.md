# Authority outreach backlog

A backlog of concrete, doable outreach targets, not executed outreach. Nothing on this list has been contacted, submitted, or published anywhere outside whatmattersbuilt.co. Each item names what to do, why it fits, and what's needed to actually do it, so it can be picked up and run without having to re-derive the plan.

## Free resources as link bait (already built, not yet promoted)

The site has 3 free resources with no email gate: [Operations KPI Checklist](https://whatmattersbuilt.co/resources/guides/operations-kpi-checklist/), [Marketing Audit Checklist](https://whatmattersbuilt.co/resources/guides/marketing-audit-checklist/), and a [ROAS calculator](https://whatmattersbuilt.co/resources/calculators/roas-calculator/). These are the actual link-worthy assets on the site right now, the case studies and field notes are good context but a checklist or calculator is what other sites actually link to.

- Backlog item: submit the ROAS calculator to free-tool directories (e.g. `tools.hunter.io`-style aggregator lists, marketing-tool roundup sites). Needs: a short description and screenshot, neither of which exist yet.
- Backlog item: post the checklists as standalone value posts on LinkedIn (not just link-outs), since LinkedIn's algorithm favors native content over outbound links. Needs: LinkedIn post copy per checklist, not yet written.

## Field Notes as guest-contribution samples

The 3 published Field Notes are real writing samples with real numbers, which is exactly what's needed to pitch guest posts, they're not generic advice, they're grounded in specific engagements. Before pitching anywhere, each target publication needs its own guidelines checked (word count, tone, whether they accept non-staff bylines) since this hasn't been done yet for any of them.

- Backlog item: identify 3 to 5 Indonesia/Southeast Asia-focused business or marketing publications that accept guest contributions, and check each one's actual submission guidelines. Not yet researched, this list doesn't exist yet.
- Backlog item: identify operations/manufacturing trade publications relevant to the Blue Tick Ice-style work (fleet compliance, factory operations), since that's a distinct audience from general marketing publications and the CRM/segmentation note. Not yet researched.

## What's deliberately not on this list

No link-buying, no PBN placement, no "guaranteed backlink" services, no directory submissions to low-quality aggregators that exist purely for SEO padding rather than real referral value. Every item above is either a real audience (a publication with actual readers) or a real utility (a tool someone would bookmark), consistent with the sprint's standing rule against manufactured authority signals.
