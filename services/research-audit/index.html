Services / Research & Audit

Research & Audit

Gap analysis and process audits that name the actual failure points, with an owner attached to every fix, not a report that gets filed and forgotten.

Who it's for
  • Businesses preparing for certification or external audit
  • Teams that suspect something's broken but haven't named it precisely
Problems it solves
  • Process failures nobody has formally owned
  • Certification paths with no clear gap analysis behind them
Scope
  • Gap analysis against the relevant standard or goal
  • Procedure documentation with a named owner per fix
  • Verification that fixes actually close the gap, not just paper over it
Not included
  • Certification issuance itself (that's the certifying body's call)
  • Legal or regulatory advice
Process
  1. Audit

    Run the gap analysis against the real standard, not an assumed one.

  2. Assign

    Attach a named owner to every fix. A fix without one doesn't survive.

  3. Verify

    Confirm each fix actually closed the gap before calling it done.

Deliverables
  • Written gap analysis
  • Procedures with named owners
  • Verified closure of each identified failure
