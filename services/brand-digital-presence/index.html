Services / Brand & Digital Presence

Brand & Digital Presence

Building a brand identity and social system from nothing — logo, visual direction, and photography that a business can actually launch on.

Who it's for
  • Founders with no existing brand identity
  • Businesses whose current identity doesn't match what they're actually selling
Problems it solves
  • No visual system to build content or campaigns on top of
  • A brand that looks generic against comparable competitors
Scope
  • Logo and identity system
  • Photography direction (including self-shot where production budget doesn't exist yet)
  • Social kit and voice guidelines
Not included
  • Paid media execution (see Performance Marketing)
  • Full brand strategy research (see Digital Strategy)
Process
  1. Verify

    Map what the business actually has versus what they think they have.

  2. Build

    Build the identity system to work with the real production constraints, not an ideal budget.

  3. Launch

    Hand over a system that can be extended without starting over.

Deliverables
  • Logo and identity system
  • Photography direction and initial assets
  • Social system ready to launch on
